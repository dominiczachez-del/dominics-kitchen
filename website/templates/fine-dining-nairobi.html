Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a private chef cost in Nairobi?

Pricing depends on guest numbers, menu complexity, location, service requirements and whether ingredients are included. Dominic's Kitchen provides quotations based on the specific booking.

Does Chef Dominic cook at my home?

Yes. Private chef services can be arranged for homes, apartments, villas, Airbnb properties, offices and suitable private venues.

Can Dominic's Kitchen shop for ingredients?

Yes. Ingredient shopping and procurement can be included. Clients can also provide their own ingredients if they prefer a service-only arrangement.

What areas of Nairobi do you serve?

Dominic's Kitchen serves Nairobi clients including Karen, Runda, Westlands, Kilimani, Lavington, Muthaiga, Gigiri, Kitisuru, Nairobi CBD and surrounding areas.

Does Dominic's Kitchen offer cooking and baking classes?

Yes. Practical cooking, baking, cake and pastry training can be arranged for individuals and small groups.
